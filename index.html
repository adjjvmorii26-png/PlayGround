FIELD GUIDE / 01

FLIGHT

You are always moving. W and S shape the throttle, the mouse steers, and A/D rolls the hull. Hold SHIFT for a warp burn — the cell drains and slowly recharges.

Scroll to pull the chase camera closer or further. C cycles camera distance.

RESONANCE

Pale echoes hum in the dark. Get close, press E, and the surrounding nebula shifts to a new palette. Six chords are hidden in the Veil. Your progress is saved locally.

SPACE releases a pulse that reveals nearby echoes and makes curious luma scatter. It costs warp energy.

LIFE

Luma are small, curious creatures. They keep a respectful distance until you drift slowly; then they gather. The Chorus is much larger, and it notices when you sing.

Golden fragments restore warp. Fly through them.

PHOTO MODE

P freezes the ship and hands you the camera. Drag to orbit and scroll to dolly. The interface falls away. Press P again to fly.

Sound: M. Help: H. Pause / release mouse: ESC.
