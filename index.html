Get on the court

Connect in four minutes.

Bring your own copy of NBA 2K16. We handle the servers, the matchmaking, and the Discord login.

  1. 01

    Install your copy

    Own NBA 2K16 on Steam and launch it once so the game files are registered locally.

    Steam~50 GBWindows 10/11
  2. 02

    Add the Reborn launcher

    Drop the launcher into your game folder. It patches matchmaking and points the client at our network.

    reborn-launcher.exe --region na-east
  3. 03

    Sign in with Discord

    No new password. Your Discord account is your identity, your rank, and your report history.

  4. 04

    Pick a region and run

    Choose the closest node, load into the park, and squad up. Cross-input is allowed and clearly labeled.

    Get the launcher in Discord
  1. 01

    Install NBA 2K16 mobile

    Use the Android build you own, including the OBB data. We never distribute game files.

    Android 9+4 GB RAMController recommended
  2. 02

    Install the Reborn Relay

    The relay app routes mobile matchmaking through our regional nodes and adds Discord sign-in.

    adb install reborn-relay.apk
  3. 03

    Link your Discord

    Approve the relay in Discord, then grant it overlay and network permissions on first launch.

  4. 04

    Choose your node

    Use Wi-Fi for ranked play. Mobile data works, but expect a higher ping and the occasional lag spike.

    Get the relay in Discord

Straight answers

Questions from the free-throw line.

Is this an official 2K server?

No. REBORN 2K16 is a volunteer-run community project. It is not affiliated with 2K, Take-Two Interactive, or the NBA. We only run matchmaking infrastructure for players who already own the game.

Do I need to own NBA 2K16?

Yes. We do not distribute game files, APKs, OBB data, or cracks. The launcher and relay only patch networking and add Discord sign-in to a copy you already own.

Does Android play with PC?

Yes, in mixed lobbies. You can also filter to same-platform only. Every lobby shows the input type and platform before the game starts, so nothing is hidden.

Is it really free?

Servers and launchers are free. Donations cover hardware and nothing is pay-to-win. Cosmetic supporter badges exist, but ratings, builds, and matchmaking are identical for everyone.

How do you handle cheaters?

Server-side checks flag impossible stats, then a human reviews the clip and match log. Bans are logged publicly in Discord and can be appealed once. Report tools live in every lobby.

Will my old MyTeam or MyPlayer carry over?

Local MyPlayer and MyTeam saves stay on your device. Online rank and season progress are tied to your Discord account, so they follow you between PC and Android.
